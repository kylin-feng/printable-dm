Condition Cards — 15 Printable Reference Cards

Fifteen cards, one per condition, each with the effect and its end-condition in plain language. Cut them out and clip them to your screen — when a player is poisoned, hand them the card.

What's inside

  • All 15 conditions — Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Grappled, Incapacitated, Invisible, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned, Unconscious and Exhaustion (6 levels).
  • Effect + end-condition on every card — what it does to you and what removes it, in two lines each.
  • Screen-fold header — fold the top band over your DM screen or binder edge so the condition name faces the players.

How to use it

Print on cardstock if you have it (regular paper plus a laminator is even better), cut along the dashed lines, and keep the deck in an envelope taped inside your screen. When a condition lands on a character, slide the card across the table to that player — they read it, they own it, and you never re-explain frightened for the fourth time.

The exhaustion card is a six-step ladder rather than a binary state, which is why it gets the same card size as the others: level, effect, and the fact that only a long rest plus food and drink removes a level.

Printing tip: Cardstock (65–80 lb / 170–220 gsm) feels great and survives greasy table hands; plain paper works fine if you plan to laminate.

FAQ

Do the cards include Exhaustion?

Yes — as a six-level ladder card, plus the other 14 standard conditions.

Are they double-sided?

Single-sided by design: fold the header band over the screen so the name shows and the text faces you.

Can players use them too?

Please do — that is the whole idea. Print a deck for the table, keep one at the screen.

What paper should I use?

Cardstock if you have it, any paper if you laminate. Cut lines are printed for scissors or a paper trimmer.
