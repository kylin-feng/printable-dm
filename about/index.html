About Printable DM

Print-first tools for Dungeon Masters, built around one idea: the best table reference is the one you can read in two seconds without unlocking anything.

Why print-first

Every sheet on this site exists because of a real moment at a real table: a skipped turn because HP was in someone's head, an argument about what Frightened does, a campaign that derailed because nobody wrote down the name of the merchant who knows where the vault is. Apps solve these problems too — until the battery dies or the laptop screen becomes a wall between the DM and the players.

So we design paper. Good paper: layouts with margins that survive hole punches, columns wide enough for adult handwriting, contrast that survives a fifty-cent office printer.

The deal

  • Every PDF is free, for personal use at your table. Print copies for your whole group.
  • No email wall, no account, no watermark, no “premium” version of the same sheet.
  • Summaries follow the 5e SRD; the sheet is a memory aid, not a replacement for the rules.

Feedback shapes the toolkit

Want a fillable version, A5 sizing, Pathfinder variants, or a sheet that does not exist yet? Tell us — the roadmap is literally the request list.
