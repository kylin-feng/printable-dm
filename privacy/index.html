Privacy Policy

Short version: we count page views anonymously and store nothing about you.

What we collect

Anonymous, aggregated counts: which page was viewed, which site referred the visit, approximate browser language, and whether the browser is new to the site (a yes/no flag stored in your own browser's local storage). No IP addresses, no fingerprints, no personal data, no advertising identifiers.

Cookies

None. The unique-visitor check uses a single local storage key that never leaves your device.

Third parties

Downloads are served from GitHub Pages together with the site. We run no ads, no analytics platforms, and no social pixels.

Questions about this policy: see the contact page.
