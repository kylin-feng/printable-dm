What's inside

  • Session header — session number, in-game date, real date, party level and who actually showed up.
  • Party status board — each PC row with HP state, conditions, inspiration and gold delta — the numbers that need carrying into next session.
  • NPCs met & names dropped — the single most-forgotten category: names, first impression, and what they want.
  • Loot, secrets & hooks — items handed out, plot points the players learned, and three blank hook boxes — because a session that ends without a hook is a session that ends the campaign.

How to use it

Fill it in during the last fifteen minutes of the session, not after — ask the players to recap out loud what happened while you write. The out-loud recap doubles as your plot-fixing window: whatever the players remember is what actually happened, regardless of your prep notes.

At the start of the next session, read the hooks box aloud as your cold open. It takes forty seconds and it re-immerses the table faster than any ‘previously on’ speech.

Printing tip: Print double-sided so the whole session lives on one physical sheet that clips into your campaign binder.

FAQ

One sheet per session?

Yes — the whole design assumes one session = one sheet in a growing campaign binder.

Does it work for online games?

It prints beautifully, but many online DMs keep it open in a tablet PDF annotator instead.

Can my players fill parts of it?

That works great — hand the loot and NPC sections to the party note-taker and keep status and hooks for yourself.

Is it free?

Yes — free PDF, no email, print as many as your campaign runs.
