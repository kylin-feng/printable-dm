What's inside

  • Page 1 — Combat flow — action economy (action / bonus action / movement / reaction / free object interaction), attack and saving throw resolution, and opportunity attack triggers.
  • Pages 2–3 — All 15 conditions — each condition in one line of effect plus one line of end-condition. Blinded through Unconscious, no page-flipping.
  • Page 4 — Common DCs & checks — the standard difficulty ladder, typical ability check examples, passive checks, cover (half / three-quarters / total) and dying rules with death save boxes.
  • Page 5 — Concentration & common snags — what breaks concentration, the DC formula, ready/delay reminders, and the grapple/shove contest rules.

How to use it

Print all four pages, fold along the middle and stand them inside your screen landscape-style: combat flow and conditions facing you at the start of the fight, DCs and concentration one flip away. If your screen is short, trim the top margin — the header band is decorative, the tables are not.

Each block is written as a ruling, not a rule citation: “Prone: melee attacks against it have advantage, ranged have disadvantage; ends by spending half its movement.” That is the whole point of a cheat sheet — the fastest path from question to “here is what happens”.

Printing tip: Choose grayscale if your table printer drinks color ink — the layout is tested to stay readable in pure black and white.

FAQ

What size screen do these inserts fit?

Pages print on US Letter / A4 and fold to fit standard landscape DM screens; trim margins for smaller folding screens.

Do the condition summaries match the official rules?

They summarize the 5e condition definitions line for line in plain language. For exact wording, the SRD is the source of truth.

Can I edit the cheat sheet?

The PDF is print-first. If you want a build-your-own version, request it on the contact page.

Is it free like the other sheets?

Yes — all four pages, one PDF, zero cost, no email required.
